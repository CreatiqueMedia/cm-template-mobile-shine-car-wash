# Mobile Shine Car Wash

> Mobile detailing website template for solo operators and fleet-ready teams.

## Live Demo

See it in action: [https://creatiquemedia.com/templates/mobile-shine-car-wash/demo](https://creatiquemedia.com/templates/mobile-shine-car-wash/demo)

## Who It Is For

Mobile car wash businesses, detailing crews, ceramic-coat services, and local fleet operators.

A conversion-first website template for mobile wash businesses with service menus, add-ons, zone-based pricing, and recurring maintenance plans.

## What's Included

**Key sections and features:**

- Service-zone pricing and travel-fee logic blocks
- Monthly membership and repeat booking paths
- Fleet inquiry funnel for commercial clients
- Before/after gallery and premium add-on upsell sections

**Additional features:**

- Instant package selector for exterior, interior, and full-detail packages
- Local route scheduling blocks
- Membership retention plans
- Dedicated B2B quote flow for fleets
- Trust builders: proof badges, review cards, insured-operator messaging
- Upsell sections for ceramic, headlight restore, and odor removal

**Demo page blocks:** Service Zone Selector, Add-On Upsell Flow, Fleet Intake Board.

## Design & Tech Highlights

Built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, with Stripe and PWA support listed in the template stack.

## Customization

Through the done-for-you white-label service, it is delivered under your brand with your zones, pricing logic, and booking flow configured.
